2. Complete Software Architecture & Source Modules

  • Station Operations Desk (/app.html): Multi-role PWA & Android TWA application supporting Owner, Manager, Salesman, and CA Viewer roles.
  • Executive Owner Portal (/owner.html): Remote God-Mode dashboard for live cash, stock, and variance monitoring.
  • Customer Self-Service Portal (/party.html): 6-digit PIN authenticated portal where fleet and B2B credit customers check live ledger statements, vehicle-wise fuel slips, and pay via UPI QR.
  • 8-Nozzle Meter & Daily Cash Engine (calc.js & app_day.js): Computes Petrol (P1–P4), Diesel (D1–D4), and Power/XP fuel sales; handles 7-digit meter rollovers automatically; deducts testing fuel (default 5L); reconciles Paytm/UPI, Card Swipe, Credit (Udhaar), Expenses (DG Generator, Driver Inaam, Chai-Khana), and Bank Deposits. Formula: Net_Closing_Cash = Opening_Cash + Gross_Fuel_Sale + Party_Cash_Jama - (Paytm + Swipe + Udhaar_Given + Total_Expenses + Bank_Deposit).
  • Double-Entry Khata & Global Chain Recalculator (app_ledger.js): Maintains running party balances alongside pinned State Bank of India (SBI) and Punjab National Bank (PNB) ledgers. Direct bank NEFT/UPI jama credits the customer ledger and mirrors to the bank ledger without altering physical cash drawer balances. Any historical edit triggers recalculateAllDaysChain() to preserve 100% mathematical parity across all dates.
  • 11-Rule Runtime Database Write-Interceptor (rules.js): Hardcoded security rulebook (R1–R11) intercepting every Firestore write: R1 blocks Viewer writes; R2-R3 lock submitted days so even the Owner must explicitly unlock before edits; R4 locks rateP, rateD, openCash, openStockP, openStockD to yesterday's closing values; R5 restricts opening balance edits; R6 prevents orphan ledger entries without a valid hisab date; R7 enforces |Litre * Rate - Amount| <= ₹1.01 and blocks future dates; R8 restricts Salesmen to active runsheet credit entries only; R9 auto-generates unique PAY- IDs; R10 keeps audit logs append-only immutable; R11 blocks day submission if closing cash is negative.
  • Fuel Physics, Dip Calibration & Tanker Shortage Audit (density.js, tanker_report.js, dip_chart.js): Uses piecewise linear dip-chart interpolation (cm to litres) and ASTM 53B 15°C standard density normalization: Rho_15C = Observed_Hydrometer + (Gamma * 1000 * (Temp_C - 15.0)), where Gamma = 0.000886 kg/L/°C for Petrol (MS) and 0.000707 kg/L/°C for Diesel (HSD). Compares received tanker volume against dip rise + sales during decantation and triggers shortage alerts if variance exceeds 25 litres.
  • 300–900 B2B Party CIBIL & Wasooli Recovery Engine (cibil.js): Scores every credit customer from 300 to 900 based on repayment speed (30%), overdue aging buckets at 15/25/35/50/70 days (20%), full balance clearances (15%), volume consistency (15%), tenure (10%), and balance ratio (10%). Automatically scales credit limits (Grade A+ [800+]: 3x limit; Grade A [700+]: 2x; Grade B [550+]: 1x; Grade C [<550]: 0.5x and auto credit hold).
  • UPI Settlement Ledger & Customer Kundali CRM (upi_hisab.js): Tracks individual QR transactions by nozzle counter, aggregates lifetime spend per customer, and triggers WhatsApp retention offers.
  • Jarvis Hinglish Voice & AI Operations Copilot (assistant.js & assistant_brain.js): Offline natural language parser supporting Hindi/Hinglish voice commands (e.g. nozzle readings, vehicle number credit entries) and predictive 7-day fuel burn-rate tank depletion alerts.
  • Multi-Tenant OMC Engine (multi_pump.js): Supports BPCL, IOCL, HPCL, and independent stations with isolated Firestore collections and dynamic brand theming.
